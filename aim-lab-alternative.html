ValTrainer · Compare

A free Aim Lab and KovaaK's alternative built for VALORANT

Aim Lab and KovaaK's are excellent general aim trainers. ValTrainer is different: it only does VALORANT, and it tries to feel exactly like it. Here is an honest comparison, including when the others are the better pick.

What ValTrainer does that a general aim trainer doesn't

  • Reads your VALORANT setup automatically: sensitivity with VALORANT's exact math, your primary, ADS and sniper crosshairs, all keybinds and your enemy highlight colour. Nothing to copy by hand.
  • VALORANT's rules: movement and acceleration, counter-strafing, jump and crouch accuracy, and all 20 guns with their own sprays and a buy menu.
  • VALORANT situations: Peek Duels, Strafe Duels, jiggle and jump peeks, site clears, post-plant, retakes and Deathmatch on true-scale map areas.
  • All 29 agents: signature drills played with each agent's own abilities, plus flash dodging.
  • An aim coach with a rank estimate, what to fix first and a 3D Rewind of your runs, plus a blind sensitivity finder and a crosshair finder.
  • Lock-In, a short research-based warm-up before ranked.

When Aim Lab or KovaaK's is the better pick

  • You play several shooters, not only VALORANT.
  • You want thousands of community scenarios, global leaderboards or structured benchmark playlists.
  • You're on a platform ValTrainer doesn't support (it's Windows-only).

Plenty of players use both: a general trainer for raw mechanics and ValTrainer for VALORANT-specific practice with their own settings. ValTrainer is free, so trying it costs nothing.

Questions

Is ValTrainer really free?

Yes. No price, no ads, no account and no paid tier. It is open-source software under the GPL-3.0.

Is ValTrainer better than Aim Lab?

It depends on what you want. For VALORANT-specific practice with your exact settings, movement, guns and agents, ValTrainer is built for that. For many games and a huge scenario library, Aim Lab or KovaaK's fit better.

Can I get banned for using it?

ValTrainer never touches VALORANT while it runs. It only reads VALORANT's settings files, the same files the game writes, and does not read game memory or inject anything.

Does it work on Mac or console?

No. ValTrainer runs on Windows 10 and 11.
